Veristar gives projects in the Stellar ecosystem clear numbers about how their sites and APIs are
used, without cookies and without storing anyone's IP address. It runs as one shared service for
the Stellar Community Fund (SCF): every project gets private dashboards of its own traffic, and
SCF operators get a cross-project view built from aggregates.

The service is free. No fees are charged to projects or to anyone else.

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Web analytics" icon="globe" href="/onboarding/web/quickstart">
    Pageviews, visitors, visits, visit duration, bounce rate, referrers, countries, devices and
    browsers from one script tag, with custom events when you want them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="API analytics" icon="server" href="/onboarding/api/quickstart">
    Request volume, status codes and latency percentiles per service, method and route, sent by
    your backend with OpenTelemetry next to the tools you already use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On-chain activity" icon="link" href="/onboarding/onchain">
    Daily operations, transactions and counterparties of your Stellar accounts and Soroban
    contracts, weekly active accounts, and calls per contract function.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Signed reports" icon="file-signature" href="/onboarding/signed-reports">
    A usage report for any period, signed with the service's published Stellar key, that anyone
    can verify without an account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Privacy by design" icon="shield-check" href="/compliance/privacy-strategy">
    No cookies, no stored IP addresses, and visitor counts based on a daily secret that is
    destroyed at midnight UTC.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open and verifiable" icon="code" href="/security/verify-tracker">
    Open source from tracker to infrastructure, with version-pinned tracker files you can check
    byte for byte.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who it is for

- **Project teams (tenants)** building on Stellar who want to understand the use of their sites,
  dapps and APIs. Start with the [tenant guides](/onboarding/overview).
- **SCF operators** who review projects and compare them on shared metrics for funding and
  ecosystem decisions. Start with the [operator guide](/operators/overview).
- **Whoever runs the service**, today or after a handover. Start with
  [deploy and operate](/deploy/overview) and the [runbooks](/runbooks/overview).

## How to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and enroll your project">
    Sign in to the console with GitHub, a Stellar wallet or a passkey, and enroll your project.
    The console shows the addresses of the service you use; the guides use the example domain
    `analytics.example`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add web analytics">
    Register a site, copy its script tag into your pages and open your dashboard. The
    [web quickstart](/onboarding/web/quickstart) walks through it, and there is a guide for each
    common framework.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add API analytics">
    Create an OTLP token and add a second exporter to your application. The
    [API quickstart](/onboarding/api/quickstart) explains the setup, and there are guides for
    Node.js, Python, Go, Rust, Java and the Stellar Anchor Platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add on-chain activity">
    Declare your Stellar accounts and Soroban contracts and prove that your project controls
    them, as described in [on-chain activity](/onboarding/onchain).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get help">
    Questions and problems go to the public issue tracker, as described in [support](/support).
  </Step>
</Steps>
