Veristar is free web, API and on-chain analytics for every project built on Stellar. You add a
script tag to your site, send OpenTelemetry from your API and declare your Stellar accounts. You
then get private dashboards of your own usage, without cookies and without stored IP addresses,
measured the same way for every project. Because every project is measured by one method, your
numbers can be trusted beyond your own team. You can share a signed usage report with anyone who
needs proof of real usage, such as a grant program, an investor or a partner, and anyone can
verify it without an account.

Veristar runs as a shared service for the Stellar ecosystem.
Stellar Community Fund (SCF) operators approve and remove projects and compare them in a cross-project view built from
aggregates, and they never see individual visitors. No fees are charged to projects or to anyone
else.

## How to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and enroll your project">
    Sign in to the console with GitHub, a Stellar wallet or a passkey, and enroll your project.
    The console shows the addresses of the service you use; the guides use the example domain
    `analytics.example`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add web analytics">
    Add a site on the **Sites** page, copy its script tag into your pages and open the **Web**
    page. The
    [web quickstart](/onboarding/web/quickstart) walks through it, and there is a guide for each
    common framework.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add API analytics">
    Create an OTLP token on the **Connect API** page and add a second exporter to your application. The
    [API quickstart](/onboarding/api/quickstart) explains the setup, and there are guides for
    Node.js, Python, Go, Rust, Java and the Stellar Anchor Platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add on-chain activity">
    Declare your Stellar accounts and Soroban contracts on the **On-chain** page and prove that your project controls
    them, as described in [on-chain activity](/onboarding/onchain).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get help">
    Questions and problems go to the public issue tracker, as described in [support](/support).
  </Step>
</Steps>
